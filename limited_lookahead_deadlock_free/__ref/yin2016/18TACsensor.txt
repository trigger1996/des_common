  Abstract—We investigate the problem of dynamic sensor
activation for decentralized decision making in partially
observed discrete-event systems, where the system  is
monitored by a set of agents. The sensors of each agent
can be turned on/off online dynamically according to a
sensor activation policy. We deﬁne a general decentralized
decision-making problem called the decentralized state
disambiguation problem, which covers the decentralized
control problem, the decentralized fault diagnosis problem,
and the decentralized fault prognosis problem. The goal is
to ﬁnd a language-based minimal sensor activation policy
for each agent such that the agents can always make a cor-
rect global decision as a team. A novel approach to solve
this problem is proposed. We adopt a person-by-person    Fig. 1.   Decentralized decision-making architecture with two agents,
